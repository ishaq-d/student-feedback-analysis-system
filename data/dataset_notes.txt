DATASET QUALITY NOTE

This project uses a synthetic student-feedback dataset. To avoid an unrealistically perfect classifier, the dataset includes a controlled annotation-ambiguity component.

- Total records: 3,000
- Records per category: 600
- Sentiment distribution: 1,860 Positive, 690 Neutral, 450 Negative
- 120 records (4% of the dataset) have their sentiment label reassigned in a balanced, deterministic manner across the three classes.
- The reassignment preserves the overall sentiment distribution and category distribution.
- The purpose is to simulate the uncertainty that can occur when sentiment is manually labelled from short or context-dependent student comments.
- Random seed: 42

The resulting model metrics must be reported exactly as measured after training; the dataset should not be altered merely to obtain a desired score.
